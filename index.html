About

Hi, I’m Fitri.

I’m a medical graduate of Universitas Mataram, now the physician in charge and company doctor at Klinik Utama Cakra Medika Maluk, West Sumbawa.

Day to day I handle occupational health examinations, and I keep deepening my work in cardiology through PERKI training.
